I reviewed the uploaded IPQC Process Audit Checklist. The checklist is already structured by process area (ESD, Through-Hole Insertion, Wave Soldering, Assembly, etc.), item number, audit requirements, time frequency, and pass/fail criteria. This makes it an excellent candidate for a QR-driven mobile audit platform.

## Vision: Smart IPQC Digital Audit System

### Current Problems

1. Paper-based audit form

   * Auditor must carry paper checklist.
   * Difficult to trace missed audits.
   * No real-time escalation.
   * No station-level analytics.

2. Audit frequency control is weak

   * Auditor may skip station.
   * No proof of actual audit location.

3. Audit findings not linked to CAPA

   * Findings remain in paper records.
   * Difficult trend analysis.

# Recommended Enhancement for Primax SMT

Rather than digitizing the entire 103-item checklist, split it into station-specific micro-audits:

| Station   | Audit Items |
| --------- | ----------- |
| ESD       | 5           |
| Printer   | 8           |
| SPI       | 6           |
| Placement | 8           |
| Reflow    | 6           |
| AOI       | 6           |
| Insertion | 6           |
| Wave      | 8           |
| Assembly  | 6           |
| Packaging | 5           |

This reduces audit time from approximately 20 minutes to less than 2 minutes per station while improving compliance and traceability significantly.

For your SMT/DIP environment, I would also add geolocation validation, QR anti-cheating logic, operator acknowledgment, and automatic PFMEA risk linkage so that repeated audit findings automatically increase occurrence ratings in the PFMEA dashboard. This creates a fully digital IATF 16949-compliant Layered Process Audit/IPQC Audit system.
